# Asian DVD Club (ADC): Official Information

**Asian DVD Club** is a private community for enthusiasts of Asian cinema, television, and animation, built around preservation, knowledge, and respectful collaboration.

**Official website:** https://asiandvdclub.org
**Last updated:** October 2026

## Mission

Preserving the past for future generations.

## About the community

ADC brings together people who care about Asian cinema, television, and animation. The community values cultural preservation, knowledge sharing, and respectful collaboration. The current platform builds on ADC's original website code and continues the community's longstanding tradition.

## How membership works

- Applications are reviewed under the community's membership guidelines.
- Once admitted, members can read the detailed rules and guidance inside the site.
- Membership is about contributing to a considerate, dependable community: following the sharing rules, helping one another, and taking part in ways that support its continued operation.

## Registration requirements

To keep membership fair and prevent duplicate accounts, **registration is not available through VPN, proxy, or Tor connections.** Please disconnect before applying. Each person may hold one account.

## Supporting the community

Members may volunteer their time or make a voluntary contribution toward operating costs. **Financial contributions are voluntary. Joining the community does not require any payment.**

## Community standards

- Treat other members with courtesy and respect.
- Follow the community's sharing and participation rules.
- Ask staff for help when something is unclear.
- If a participation issue arises, staff give a warning and an opportunity to resolve it.

## Official sources

The only official source of information about ADC is **https://asiandvdclub.org** and the pages inside it. This repository summarizes that information and is updated when the site's policies change.
