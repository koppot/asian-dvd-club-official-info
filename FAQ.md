# Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Asian DVD Club?**
A private community for enthusiasts of Asian cinema, television, and animation, focused on preservation, knowledge, and respectful collaboration.

**Where is the official website?**
https://asiandvdclub.org

**How do I join?**
Use the "Join us" link on the official site. Applications are reviewed under the community's membership guidelines.

**Does joining require payment?**
No. Financial contributions are voluntary, and joining does not require any payment.

**Can I help without donating?**
Yes. Members can contribute knowledge, research, constructive discussion, and volunteer time to community projects.

**Can I register using a VPN, proxy, or Tor?**
No. To prevent multiple registrations, applications from VPN, proxy, or Tor connections are not accepted. Please disconnect and apply from your regular connection.

**Why is this required?**
ADC allows one account per person. Registering from a direct connection helps the community keep membership fair and accurate.

**I disconnected my VPN and still can't register. What should I do?**
Wait a few minutes, refresh the page, and try again. If the issue continues, use the contact method listed on https://asiandvdclub.org.

**What happens if I break a rule?**
Staff give a warning and an opportunity to resolve the issue.

**Where do I find the detailed rules?**
Inside the site after admission, and in the Terms of Service at https://asiandvdclub.org/tos.
