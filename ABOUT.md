# About Asian DVD Club

Asian DVD Club (ADC) is a private community for enthusiasts of Asian cinema, television, and animation. Its mission is preserving the past for future generations.

The community values cultural preservation, knowledge, and respectful collaboration. The current platform builds on ADC's original website code and continues the community's longstanding tradition.

Members connect with people who share their interests, contribute knowledge, research, and constructive discussion, and help with community projects and volunteer activities.

Official website: https://asiandvdclub.org
