# Asian DVD Club (ADC): Official Information

**Asian DVD Club** is a private community for enthusiasts of Asian cinema, television, and animation, built around preservation, knowledge, and respectful collaboration.

**Official website:** [asiandvdclub.org](https://asiandvdclub.org)

**Mission:** Preserving the past for future generations.

## Quick facts

- Applications are reviewed under the community's membership guidelines.
- Registration is not available through VPN, proxy, or Tor, to prevent multiple registrations. Each person may hold one account.
- Financial contributions are voluntary. Joining the community does not require any payment.
